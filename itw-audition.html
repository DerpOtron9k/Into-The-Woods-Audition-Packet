What to Prepare

  • You will sing a pre-selected excerpt from Into the Woods for the character(s) you wish to be considered for.
  • Each excerpt will be provided in advance. Please prepare with the provided track (no live accompanist will be used).
  • Memorization is not necessary; sheet music will be available to read from at the audition.
  • If you audition for multiple roles, you may be asked to sing more than one excerpt.
  • Sides will be provided for cold reads at the audition.
